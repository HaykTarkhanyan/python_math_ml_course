Lightcast, "Four Takeaways from the 2026 Stanford AI Index" (13 Apr 2026) 2.5% of all US job postings mention AI skills, up 55% from the year before and nearly 300% over a decade. "Agentic AI" skills went from 0.06% of postings in 2024 to 0.23% in 2025, a more than 280% rise, nearly 90,000 US postings. Python was the most in-demand specialized skill (258,674 postings). Share of postings asking for AI skills in 2025: Singapore 4.769%, Hong Kong 3.5%, Luxembourg 3.4%, Spain 3.3%, US 2.6%. Good: Lightcast supplies the AI Index's job-posting data. Numbers quoted from the blog page itself; the full AI Index chapter was not read.
AGBU Magazine, "Jobs of Tomorrow, Today" (June 2026, Lusine Minasyan) Armenian IT firms now check whether AI can do a task before opening a role, and hiring has shifted toward customer service, support, sales and operations. "Demand remains for data analysts and QAs, but these require pre-existing technical foundations." Finance, banking, sales and service dominate the top professions of 2025. Unemployment around 12% in 2025; nearly 90% of jobs in Yerevan (2023 to 2025). Interviews and a magazine article, not a dataset. Matches what staff.am looks like: banks dominate it.
365 Data Science, ML engineer skills and AI engineer outlook (2026) About 1,000 US ML engineer postings (Glassdoor): Python first, then SQL and Java; PyTorch and TensorFlow lead the deep-learning libraries. Of 903 AI engineer postings, only 2.5% target 0 to 2 years of experience. Vendor content (they sell courses); from search summaries, pages not re-read in full.
Axial Search, AI engineering jobs market map (July 2026) About 43,500 US AI engineering postings since January 2026, roughly 1,550 new ones a week, holding up while wider tech hiring softened. Recruiting-firm content; from a search summary only.
Jobicy, ML specialist salary in Armenia (June 2026) Average about $23,000 a year; junior $13,800 to $18,400, senior $27,600 to $41,400. Salary aggregator, method unknown. A rough order of magnitude at best.
