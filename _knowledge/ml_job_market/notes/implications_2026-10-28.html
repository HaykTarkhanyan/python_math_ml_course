Written 2026-10-28 from this snapshot. Read it with the limits in section 2: the Armenian ML/AI/DS group is 74 postings from 23 companies, and EPAM has 35 of them. Shares below are share of companies unless they say otherwise.

Five things the data says

  1. Most Armenian "ML" hiring right now is AI engineering, not model building. Of the 74 postings, 50 have an AI-engineer or GenAI title; 24 are ML engineer, data scientist, MLOps, computer vision or research. The AI-engineer jobs ask for Python plus LLM provider APIs, agents, RAG and MCP inside an ordinary software team. That is stream B's content almost one to one.
  2. The biggest gap against a typical ML course is software engineering, and it holds for classic ML titles too. ML engineer and data scientist titles only, Armenia vs worldwide: Git 53% vs 9%, CI/CD 53% vs 13%, Docker 47% vs 19%, MLOps 47% vs 31%. (15 Armenian companies against 78, so wide error bars; the direction is the finding, not the decimals.)
  3. Agents and MCP are already in Armenian job ads. Agents: 52% of Armenian companies. MCP: 8 of 23 (Acba Bank, DataArt, Provectus, EPAM, CloudLinux, Playrix, Libertex, UltaHost). Worldwide postings found by the AI-engineer / LLM-engineer searches: agents 83%, RAG 76%, LangChain/LangGraph 59%, fine-tuning 55%, guardrails 52%, vector databases 51%, evals 49%.
  4. Statistics and classic ML are asked less in Armenia than worldwide (same like-for-like titles: statistics 33% vs 55%, scikit-learn 20% vs 36%, TensorFlow 20% vs 44%, time series 0% vs 18%). The banks are the exception: Ameriabank's data scientist and ML validator postings ask for statistics, probability and gradient boosting, and Ameriabank alone has five ML postings open.
  5. Entry-level ML jobs barely exist. 4 of the 74 are entry level (Acba Bank's Agentic AI Engineer, Provectus' Junior AI/ML Engineer, research new-grad roles at NVIDIA and Commit); 58 are senior; the median minimum is 4 years. The realistic first doors are data analyst roles (31 postings; SQL at 96% of their companies, BI tools 61%, Python 57%) and Armenian-language AI data work (AI training and evaluation gigs, e.g. LILT AI, Productive Playhouse).
