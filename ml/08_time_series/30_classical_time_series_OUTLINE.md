## Sections / frames

1. **Cold open** — "every model so far assumed rows were interchangeable"; time
   series breaks it. Two-lecture roadmap.
2. **What makes time series different** — vocabulary (horizon, uni/multivariate);
   the four components (trend/season/cycle/noise); STL decomposition. *(figs:
   ts_anatomy, stl_decomposition)*
3. **Stationarity and differencing** — definition; stationary vs 3 non-stationary
   panels; differencing with **ADF and KPSS** (opposite nulls); **predict-first:
   is a second difference better?** — no, the spread goes 40.9 → 10.8 → **11.7**.
   *(figs: stationarity, differencing, over_differencing)*
4. **Autocorrelation** — ACF vs PACF on a clean AR(2); then the
   **MA(q)-is-not-a-rolling-mean** frame, because lecture 31 builds rolling-mean
   features two frames later and the name collision bites every year.
   *(fig: acf_pacf)*
5. **ARIMA family** — AR, MA, ARMA, ARIMA(p,d,q), SARIMA(p,d,q)(P,D,Q)_m, and
   the one-season-only limit. Then orders read off **our own series**: raw ACF
   decays slowly, and after (1−B)(1−B¹²) a single negative lag-12 spike ⇒ seasonal
   MA ⇒ the **airline model** (0,1,1)(0,1,1)₁₂. Confirmed against the old guess by
   AIC (343.8 vs 356.4) and test MAE (6.07 vs 7.24).
   *(figs: acf_pacf_series, arima_forecast)*
6. **Exponential smoothing** — ETS = Error/Trend/Seasonality; SES → Holt →
   Holt-Winters. *(fig: exp_smoothing)*
7. **Baselines and evaluation** — naive / seasonal-naive / drift;
   MAE/RMSE/MAPE/MASE spelled out; **MASE worked by hand** (6/18 = 0.33), which is
   also where the in-sample denominator gets explained; time-ordered holdout;
   **even the baseline has to respect the arrow** — `shift(12)` past one season
   reads inside the test window, and fixing it moves seasonal naive from MASE 0.90
   to 1.22. *(fig: time_aware_split)*
8. **Recap** + green "Next: ML for time series" box.
