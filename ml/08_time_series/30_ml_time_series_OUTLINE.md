## Sections / frames

1. **Cold open** — "where we left off"; reuse our regressors on a series by making
   it a table.
2. **Forecasting as supervised learning** — sliding-window → (X, y) design matrix.
   *(fig: supervised_reframe)*
3. **Leakage and time-aware splitting** — predict-first: random split = leakage;
   random vs forward split. *(fig: time_aware_split)*
4. **Feature engineering for time** — four families: lags, (shifted) rolling,
   calendar/Fourier, exogenous. Golden rule = knowable-at-t.
5. **Time-aware cross-validation** — `TimeSeriesSplit` forward-chaining, now with
   **`max_train_size`** (rolling window) and **`gap`** (a `rolling(7)` feature at
   the start of validation was built from the last training days). *(fig: ts_cv,
   three panels)*
6. **Tree models and the extrapolation trap** — predict-first: gradient boosting
   flat-lines on a trend; the fix is to predict the difference (MAE 19.0 → 6.7);
   recursive vs direct multi-step. *(fig: gbm_forecast)*
7. **Industry standards** — what people actually run. Prophet as a *curve*, not a
   process (Taylor & Letham, 2018): `y = g(t) + s(t) + h(t) + ε`, with no lag
   anywhere, so gaps and irregular spacing do not break it. Then the piecewise-linear
   trend (25 candidate changepoints over the first 80% of history, sparse prior,
   `changepoint_prior_scale` 0.05) and its sharp edge — **the forecast is the last
   segment extended**, which in the figure finishes 37 units low. Then Fourier
   seasonality (RMSE 4.7 → 3.5 → 1.7 at orders 1/3/10; defaults 10 yearly, 3 weekly)
   and **many seasonalities at once**, which answers the one-`m` limit lecture 29
   left open. Then the honest verdict (fpp3 §12.2: worse than both ETS and ARIMA on
   their example, residual autocorrelation leaving intervals too narrow) and the
   production stack (Nixtla, sktime, Darts, skforecast, GluonTS).
   *(figs: prophet_components, prophet_changepoints, fourier_seasonality)*
8. **Deep learning and foundation models** — DeepAR/N-BEATS/TFT/PatchTST lineage;
   foundation models (TimesFM, Chronos-2, Moirai-2, TimeGPT, Sundial, Time-MoE)
   with GIFT-Eval / ProbTS as the leaderboards and a "try the simple thing first"
   reality check. *(fig: deep_ts_timeline)*
9. **Recap** + toolbox list (sktime, statsforecast/mlforecast, skforecast, Darts,
   Prophet, GluonTS).
